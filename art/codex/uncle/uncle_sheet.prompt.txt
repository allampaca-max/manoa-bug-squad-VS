Character: the friendly next-door neighbour, a tall slim Korean man in his forties, short black hair, rectangular black-framed glasses, a calm cheerful face, plain white crew-neck T-shirt, khaki knee-length shorts, grey slippers. In his right hand he always holds a folded white kitchen paper towel. Cute chibi proportions like the attached sprites, but clearly taller and lankier than the girl.
The attached style image shows other sprites from the same game (the girl on the left) for style and scale.

Size: in every frame the whole man is about 30 art pixels wide and 62 art pixels tall. Clean 1-art-pixel near-black outline, 3-4 shades per colour, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no gradients.

Layout: 4 rows of frames, read left to right. Every frame faces RIGHT, shows the full body from head to slippers, and is the same size. Leave at least 8 art pixels of empty space between frames and rows. Feet of all frames in a row sit on the same line.
- Row 1, idle, 3 frames: standing relaxed, paper towel in hand, a small breathing bob and one blink.
- Row 2, swipe, 4 frames: bends forward and swipes the paper towel low across the floor in front of him in a quick sweeping motion, then lifts it up with a satisfied look (no bug drawn).
- Row 3, startled, 2 frames: leans back in surprise with his free hand raised, glasses slightly askew.
- Row 4, walk, 6 frames: brisk walk cycle to the right, long legs stepping alternately, towel hand swinging.

Render: every art pixel is an exact square block of 4x4 image pixels. Landscape image. Fully transparent background (if impossible, one flat pure magenta #FF00FF). No text, no labels, no grid lines, no shadows, no border.
